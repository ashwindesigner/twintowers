Screen 01
Login & Authentication
Secure OTP-based sign-in with branded landing experience.
  • Mobile OTP authentication
  • Email login fallback
  • Community branding front and center
  • New resident registration
Screen 02
Community Dashboard
At-a-glance view of everything happening in your community.
  • Personalised welcome & flat info
  • Live issue & poll summary
  • Upcoming event banner
  • 7 quick-access service tiles
  • Recent issues feed
Screen 03
Maintenance & Asset Records
Full condition tracking across towers, clubhouse, parking and common areas.
  • Status per asset: Good / Fair / Poor
  • Filter by Tower A, B, Clubhouse, Parking
  • Warranty expiry alerts
  • Full inspection history timeline
Screen 04
Issues & Tracking
Community-wide ticketing system with full lifecycle management.
  • Raise issues with photos
  • Filter by status & priority
  • SLA due dates & assignments
  • Comment thread on each ticket
  • High / Medium / Low priority
Screen 05
Cultural Activities
Events, festival proposals and photo gallery all in one place.
  • Upcoming events with registration
  • Resident event proposals
  • Budget voting & approval flow
  • Photo gallery by event

Facility Slot Booking
Self-service booking for all community amenities.
  • Badminton courts, lawn, banquet hall
  • 7-day date picker
  • Live availability grid
  • Booking confirmation with ID
  • Max 2 active bookings per flat
Screen 07
Policies & Rules
Official community guidelines — clear, accessible and always up to date.
  • Pool, parking, noise, clubhouse rules

Screen 08
Polls & Voting
Democratic decision-making for the entire community.
  • Elections, budgets, policy decisions

Notifications
